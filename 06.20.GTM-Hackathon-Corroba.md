## What we built
Corroba (originally called **ProofLoop**) turns the customer proof a company already has into growth material. That proof is usually buried in testimonials, reviews, support tickets, NPS surveys, sales call transcripts and customer emails. Tagline: *"Turn hidden customer proof into your growth engine."* The core idea is proof *discovery*: find, score and rank the strongest trust signals (for example "We saved £40,000"), then use them for audience targeting, content and a go-to-market playbook. Generating content comes second.

### What it does
- **Proof discovery**: upload PDFs, CSVs or TXT files, or paste testimonials, emails and transcripts. It pulls out trust signals such as revenue or time savings, growth gains, risk reduction and emotional moments.
- **Proof Score & rankings**: scores each signal on specificity, credibility and impact, then ranks which proof is strongest.
- **Social proof validation**: checks a quote against public social posts (LinkedIn, X, Instagram...) and marks it verified, partial or unverified.
- **Audience expansion**: given a strong proof quote, suggests the companies and ICPs it would resonate with most (e.g. "saved recruiters 12 hours/week" → staffing agencies, talent platforms).
- **Auto-GTM generator**: one click turns the top 3 signals into a GTM playbook (ICP, positioning, outreach sequence, content ideas, landing-page angles, growth loops), and users can rate it as helpful or not.
- **Content & growth recommendations**: generates proof-led content assets and a ranked list of next actions by impact/effort.
- **Proof CRM**: syncs signals and generated assets to a CRM.
- **Demo mode**: ships with realistic sample data (G2 review, sales call, support tickets, NPS CSV...) so judges get value within 60 seconds with no API keys.

### How it's put together
- **Next.js 16 + React 19 web app** (`apps/web`): dashboard with pages for Discovery, Validation, Trust Signals, Rankings, Audiences, Content, GTM, Growth, CRM, Analytics and Unify; Tailwind v4 styling and D3 charts/network visuals.
- **Hono API on Node** (`apps/api`): REST endpoints for sources, discovery, signals, GTM, content, CRM and growth, with rate limiting and Zod validation.
- **Extraction pipeline**: regex/heuristic signal extraction and scoring, upgraded to OpenAI extraction when a key is set.
- **RAG layer**: chunking, an in-memory vector store and Voyage AI embeddings over the demo sources, used to ground extraction and audience expansion.
- **PostgreSQL + Drizzle ORM**: schema, migrations and seed scripts, including a Zero sync table.
- **Sponsor integrations**: each one runs live when its API key is set and falls back to a simulated/demo version otherwise.
- **Infra**: Docker Compose (Postgres, Typesense, Grafana, OpenTelemetry collector), Dockerfile + Fly.io config for the API (London region), Vercel config for the frontend.
